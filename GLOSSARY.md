# Glossary

**Item** — Anything in the catalog that someone might have at home and an Idea can use: leftovers to rescue (banana peel, coffee grounds) as well as pantry staples (honey, oats). Belongs to one Category.

**Category** — The material group an Item belongs to (e.g. food, plastic).

**Idea** — A way to reuse an Item, of one Idea kind (kitchen, beauty, cleaning, garden, home, crafts).

**Ingredient** — An Item, seen either as something the person picked into their Ingredient list or as something an Idea needs. Not a separate concept: every Ingredient is an Item. An Idea's own Item is always one of its Ingredients.

**Key Ingredient** — An Ingredient an Idea cannot be made without. Only Key Ingredients count towards a Match.

**Basics** — Common things assumed to be at home (salt, oil, water, sugar, spices). An Idea mentions them but never counts them as missing.

**Ingredient list** — The Ingredients a person has picked to find Ideas they can make. Temporary: it lasts for the current app session, not across launches.

**Match** — An Idea that uses at least one Ingredient from the Ingredient list. Grouped by how many of the Idea's Ingredients are missing:
- **Make it now** — nothing missing.
- **Almost there** — one missing.
- **Save for later** — two or more missing.

**Start making** — Following an Idea's steps one at a time, with progress shown.
_Avoid_: "recipe" for an Idea in general. A kitchen Idea is still an Idea.
